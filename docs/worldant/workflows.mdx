---
title: Workflows
description: Durable deterministic orchestration and internal Step checkpoints.
---

A Workflow is a default-exported async TypeScript function under `workflows/` or
`apps/<app>/workflows/`.

Workflow bodies are deterministic orchestration. They may call Steps, call child Workflows, branch
on recorded values, and use durable wait primitives. They may not directly access databases,
network, filesystem, ambient timers, randomness, or other nondeterministic capabilities.

Steps live under `workflows/steps/` or `apps/<app>/workflows/steps/`. They are internal Workflow IO
checkpoints and are never public callables.

Step database effects, transaction-bound Workflow scheduling, Step output, and durable completion
commit atomically in one kernel-owned transaction. `db.transaction` is unavailable in Steps.

Client usage uses two awaits:

```ts
const run = await world.todo.remind(input)
const output = await run.handle
```
